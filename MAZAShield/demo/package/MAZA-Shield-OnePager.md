# MAZA Shield

### Explainable Risk Intelligence for Casino Operations

---

**MAZA Shield** is MAZALab’s decision layer for casino risk: it unifies entity intelligence, multi-signal **gaming-domain** scoring, and **operator-ready** outputs in a single assessment flow—so fraud, AML, vendor, bonus, and responsible-gaming desks see the **same story** the engine used, not a black-box score.

Ship as an API-first foundation today, demo with a **G2E-ready** dashboard: live `POST /assess`, comparative scenarios, adaptive **combos**, an **operator playbook** aligned to priority and lane, and an **append-only audit** trail you can export and defend in review.

---

## Key benefits

- **One contract, full stack** — Resolve → score → assess → audit under one correlation id; batch and per-entity views for rings, syndicates, and VIP cohorts.
- **Explainable by design** — Top drivers, dimension narratives, and gaming **advanced patterns** (where configured) with templates, SLAs, and console pivots—not a single opaque number.
- **Operator playbook built in** — Priority-tagged actions (`[Gaming-P1…P4]`), escalation lanes, artefact checklists, and batch-level recommendations when signals align.
- **Governance you can stand behind** — Structured logging, dual JSON/SQLite audit persistence, stats and correlation-id lookup for **traceability** from UI to log.
- **Additive roadmap** — Gaming enrichment and new domains extend signal without breaking published assessment shapes.

## MAZA Shield vs QCI (compliance-rules–style stack)

| Dimension | QCI-style / rules-heavy compliance tools | **MAZA Shield** |
|-----------|------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Primary lens** | Policy checks, lists, thresholds, ticket routing | **Composite risk** + **cross-dimension** patterns (e.g. adaptive combos) + explainable drivers |
| **Output** | Pass/fail, cases, workflow status | **Graded risk**, **drivers**, **playbook lines**, optional **advanced pattern** payloads for ops |
| **Explainability** | Often rule-id or checklist oriented | **Analyst-grade** narratives, contributions, and artefact hooks tied to dimensions |
| **Casino floor fit** | Strong for **pure compliance** gates | Built for **floor + AML + fraud + RG** triage in one assessment **batch** |
| **Audit & evidence** | Case history in the compliance tool | **Append-only audit**, exportable narratives, correlation lineage API-side |

*“QCI” denotes typical **compliance-first** intelligence platforms; vendor capabilities vary.*
